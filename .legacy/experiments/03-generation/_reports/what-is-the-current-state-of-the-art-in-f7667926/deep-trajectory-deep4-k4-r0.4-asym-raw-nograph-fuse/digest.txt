DEEP CRITIQUES (full-text peer review of key papers):
arXiv:2603.27950:
NOVELTY: Unifies generative and hallucination paradigms by applying test-time compute scaling (e.g., Beam Search, MCTS) to a flow-based atomistic generative prior.
STRENGTHS: Achieves SOTA in-silico success for protein and small molecule targets, significantly outperforming hallucination methods like BindCraft under normalized compute budgets (Fig 7).
WEAKNESSES: Validation relies entirely on in-silico proxies (fpLDDT, fipAE); furthermore, the Teddymer dataset is purely synthetic, derived from predicted AFDB structures, which may propagate existing model biases.
RELEVANCE: Advances the field by demonstrating that protein binder design can be treated as a scalable inference problem, moving beyond the dichotomy of pure generation versus pure optimization.
arXiv:2503.13522:
NOVELTY: A comprehensive taxonomic synthesis of the transition from traditional homology/MD methods to modern generative diffusion models in both structure prediction and design.
STRENGTHS: Granular technical decomposition of AlphaFold2 and AlphaFold3 architectures, specifically detailing the shift from Evoformer to Pairformer and the integration of Diffusion modules.
WEAKNESSES: The text functions as an exhaustive survey/compilation rather than an original research contribution; it lacks novel experimental validation or a new algorithmic proposal.
RELEVANCE: Directly maps the current state-of-the-art by categorizing AlphaFold3's multi-chain capabilities alongside sequence-based (ProteinMPNN) and structure-based design tools.
arXiv:2504.01490:
NOVELTY: Synthesizes the "predict-design-validate" paradigm by mapping the technological convergence of DeepMind’s prediction models and Baker Lab’s generative design tools.
STRENGTHS: Provides granular architectural comparisons, specifically detailing AlphaFold3’s transition to the Pairformer module and RFDiffusion’s multi-scale noise scheduling (Gaussian and ISO(3)).
WEAKNESSES: The review is primarily descriptive; it lacks a rigorous critical analysis of model failure modes or a quantitative comparison of the proposed "hybrid physics-AI" solutions.
RELEVANCE: Directly addresses the state-of-the-art by bifurcating the field into structure prediction (AlphaFold/RoseTTAFold) and de novo design (RFDiffusion/ProteinMPNN) workflows.
