\section{Preprocessing (01-*) --- all SQL-cheap, descriptive}

\subsection*{01-01 resolver quality}
Each bibliography entry is resolved to an arXiv id through a ladder of matchers: literal link
$\to$ regex $\to$ DOI $\to$ journal-ref $\to$ exact title $\to$ fuzzy title. This counts which
rung resolved each of the 177M citation occurrences, by field and era. Found: 40.2\% of
citations resolve to arXiv at all (the rest cite journals/books outside the corpus); ${\sim}80\%$
of resolutions come from the exact rungs; the fuzzy tail is bounded at ${\sim}11\%$. The graph
is not built on guesses.
\rerun{Corpus grew by 595k parsed papers today; numbers drift. Cheap; refresh for any public table.}

\subsection*{01-02 labeling quality}
Every citation edge is tagged with \emph{why} the citing paper cites
(extends/uses/compares/background/critiques/motivates) plus polarity. A 26B teacher labeled
100k samples; a DistilBERT student distilled from it labels the full 65M+ resolved edges. This
measures the student on held-out teacher labels: polarity essentially solved (acc $0.95$);
intent acc $0.84$, macro-F1 $0.72$; the rare \texttt{critiques} class lags ($0.58$).
\rerun{Frozen artifacts; a rerun reproduces itself. Only meaningful after retraining the student.}

\subsection*{01-03 graph structure}
A census of the resolved intent-typed graph: 36.7M distinct edges over 3.07M papers, degree
distributions, growth, top-cited list. Sanity anchor: in-degree ranking recovers the canonical
landmarks (ResNet/BERT/Transformer). Documents the heavy-tailed hub problem that motivates
``induce within a topical pool.''
\rerun{+1.7M edges today; cheap census refresh.}

\subsection*{01-04 clustering comparison}
The corpus topic tree is built by recursive bisection of the embedding cloud with a coherence
early-stop. Pitted against k-means / DBSCAN / HDBSCAN on a common 30k sample: HDBSCAN dumps
59\% of papers into a noise bucket, DBSCAN collapses to one blob; the tree places every paper
(0\% noise) at density-adaptive grain. Silhouette is the wrong lens at this grain.
\rerun{Algorithm-choice conclusion, not data-dependent. No.}

\subsection*{01-05 topic tree}
Shape census of the shipped tree: 3{,}582 leaves over 2.92M papers, leaf-size/depth
distributions, coherence-vs-size scatter. Confirms the early-stop keeps leaves tight.
\rerun{The tree is frozen \emph{by design} and now ends ${\sim}180$k papers before the corpus
does; the interesting action is a phase-5 \texttt{--include-force} rebuild decision, not this
census.}

\section{Retrieval (02-*) --- picking the papers a review is built from}

\subsection*{02-01 subgraph retrieval}
The core recipe: embed the question; pull a candidate pool (vector KNN fused with lexical
BM25-style match); add each pool paper's most-cited ancestors; keep only citation edges inside
the pool (\emph{induce}); rank by personalized PageRank
\begin{equation}
\pi \;=\; (1-\alpha)\, e_q \;+\; \alpha\, P^{\!\top} \pi ,
\end{equation}
where $e_q$ concentrates restart mass on query-similar nodes. Tested against PCST and
similarity-BFS selectors on expert-canonical landmark sets: recall $0.742$ of canonical
landmarks, $0.917$ of the foundational spine, against a $0.85$ pool ceiling --- dominates every
recall axis; PCST wins only connectivity (a presentation concern).
\rerun{\stale Ran under the \texttt{ef\_search}=40 cap and pre-fusion, i.e.\ handicapped; a
rerun on the fixed stack should score \emph{higher}. Rerun if this number goes in the paper.}

\subsection*{02-02 relevance judge}
Recall cannot see junk. An LLM labels every selected landmark's role
(foundational/core/frontier/peripheral/unrelated) across 27 topics: ${\sim}78\%$ on-topic, and
the diagnosis that the ${\sim}22\%$ junk is famous cross-field hubs whose in-degree (5.4, as
high as core papers) makes them invisible to PPR as noise.
\rerun{\stale Historical ``before'' picture; 02-05 measured the same quantity at $0.945{\to}1.0$
after the floor + fusion shipped. Keep as history.}

\subsection*{02-03 ancestor gate}
The junk enters through one door: ancestor injection. Sweep a cosine admission floor
\begin{equation}
\text{admit ancestor } a \iff \cos\!\big(u_q, u_a\big) \ge \tau, \qquad \tau \in \{0.25,\dots,0.45\},
\end{equation}
on both precision (27 topics) and gold recall (3 topics). Recall is flat on $[0.25,0.35]$
($0.808$, spine $1.0$) while precision rises monotonically $\Rightarrow$ shipped $\tau=0.35$ as
the max-precision edge of the plateau; $\tau \ge 0.40$ cuts genuinely foundational low-cosine
papers (recall collapses to $0.522$ at $0.45$).
\rerun{\stale\stale Superseded twice: 02-07 moved production to $\tau=0.30$ (the reader now does
the junk-filtering job) and the sweep ran under the ef=40 bug. Methodology stands; number retired.}

\subsection*{02-04 selection flags}
Given a pool, pick the final set: fixed top-50 vs cosine-gated vs LLM-filtered (same count) vs
LLM-filtered-and-bigger, scored by per-paper LLM grades in $[-2,2]$. Filtering lifts both sum
(+42\%) and mean (+40\%); going bigger lifts only sum while the mean saturates at ${\sim}0.88$
--- the same place the compose judge saturates. Filter $\gg$ count.
\rerun{Absorbed into the fusion design; historical.}

\subsection*{02-05 reader fusion}
Measure-gate for the shipped default. The production reader grades every candidate (6-class
role + relevance $r\in[-2,2]$, cached by question hash); ranking fuses semantics and grade:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{score}_i \;=\; \cos\!\big(u_q, u_i\big)\cdot \frac{r_i + 2}{4},
\qquad r_i = -2 \Rightarrow \text{excluded from landmarks, kept as graph connector.}
\end{equation}
Gate: gold recall (grader-independent) must not regress. Result: recall identical
($0.808$ / spine $1.0$), unrelated landmarks $5.7\%\to 0\%$, freed slots shift to core
($0.270\to0.307$) and frontier ($0.224\to0.257$). Pareto win; shipped as \texttt{fuse: true}.
\rerun{Current, load-bearing default. Rerun only to refresh numbers on the grown corpus.}

\subsection*{02-06 selection objective}
PPR itself on trial (motivated by real misses: SimPO/KTO never became landmarks). Six
objectives over an identical front-end: PPR vs facility location
$\max_S \sum_{p\in\mathrm{pool}} \max_{s\in S}\, \mathrm{sim}(p,s)$, MMR, cluster-proportional,
fused-topk, hybrid. Verdict: \textbf{PPR vindicated} --- gold recall $0.753/0.917$ vs
$0.30$--$0.44$ for every set-coverage arm (facility $0.436$; re-checked on uncapped pools:
$0.297$). The rich-get-richer bias \emph{is} the mechanism that recovers foundational lineage;
precision is already handled by fusion (relevance $1.0$ on every arm). Autopsy of real misses
$\Rightarrow$ three causes: pool-miss, lukewarm grades on question-named entities, budget
pressure --- none fixed by the objective.
\rerun{Current; verdict strengthened on the fixed stack. Do not relitigate \emph{vs
set-coverage}. Note 07-17: this verdict is about objectives; the magnet-walk trajectory hybrid
(PPR scaffold + 8 consensus-walk slots, a different successor) now beats pure PPR $6/6$ on the
external survey benchmark (04-04) and is prod selection in the MCP path.}

\subsection*{02-07 retrieval breadth (the bug + the coupled package)}
pgvector's HNSW returns at most \texttt{hnsw.ef\_search} rows regardless of \texttt{LIMIT}:
\begin{equation}
|\mathrm{KNN}(q, k)| \;\le\; \min(k,\ \texttt{ef\_search}),
\qquad \texttt{ef\_search}_{\text{default}} = 40 ,
\end{equation}
and the parameter was never set --- every vector KNN since the PG consolidation returned
$\le 40$ rows; \texttt{n\_pool}=400/600 was fiction and lexical RRF silently carried retrieval
(fatal for long questions: litmus pools 60--137). Fix: \texttt{SET hnsw.ef\_search = 1000} in
\texttt{Store.\_\_init\_\_}. Then the coupled package: ef alone \emph{drops} landmark hits
(bigger pools make $m{=}40$ more competitive), so ship $\{$ef $1000$, floor $0.30$, $m{=}60\}$
together; floor $0.30$ at $m{=}40$ dilutes gold ($0.753\to0.711$) --- never the floor without
the budget. Grading cost scales with $m$ only ($\text{candidates}=4m$), not pool size. Result:
litmus pool-hit $0.857\to1.0$, landmark-hit $0.524\to{\sim}0.62$, gold gate held $0.753/0.917$;
$m{=}80$ documented as the paid tier. BYOL is the emblem: vocabulary-shift foundational paper,
KNN rank $>1000$, reachable only through the ancestor channel, blocked by $\tau=0.35$ by $0.010$.
\rerun{\textbf{THE regression harness.} Rerun after any retrieval change and to refresh on the
+27k corpus.}

\section{Generation (03-*) + flagship}

\subsection*{03-01 judge validity}
Validates the measurement instrument: plant known defects, check detection per axis; test
position and verbosity bias. Sound: 100\% on known facts, catches coverage/grounding
degradation, no biases; lineage is the weakest axis. Rule: trust grounding/coverage and large
effects; asterisk tiny lineage margins.
\rerun{Judge gained retrieval-context + dated preambles in July; a light re-validation is
defensible, not urgent.}

\subsection*{03-02 component ablation}
Knock out one component at a time (PPR$\to$in-degree / no-cluster / no-graph / no-fulltext) on
the 27-topic battery, reading the full metric vector + 4-axis judge + faithfulness tripwire.
The suite's founding lesson: \emph{read the vector, never one number} --- \texttt{no\_graph}
posts the best frontier count while zeroing edges, recall, and judge score.
\rerun{\stale Ran on the pre-fusion, pre-ef-fix, sym-era stack. The paper-grade ablation table
wants a rerun on the current stack.}

\subsection*{03-03 embedding format}
Query-encoding shootout, end-to-end: $\{$sym, asym$\}\times\{$HyDE, raw$\}$. \texttt{asym\_raw}
ties for best recall ($0.81$ / spine $1.0$) at no extra cost; HyDE only rescues the symmetric
space. Shipped --- and it is why asym is now the prod space.
\rerun{Settled; re-open only post-LoRA (the v3 training gates on this comparison anyway).}

\subsection*{03-04 recency knob}
Sweep the lineage$\leftrightarrow$frontier dial $0.0$--$0.8$ (frontier slots reserved
newest-cohort-first; age-normalized score $\approx$ citations per year,
$c/(y_{\mathrm{now}}-y+1)$). Frontier landmarks climb monotonically $3.7\to30.8$ at constant
grounding: a true control surface with no universal optimum --- correctly a per-query knob.
\rerun{Structural behavior; no.}

\subsection*{03-05 frontier pull}
The topic tree predates the corpus delta, so tree-anchored retrieval can never surface post-tree
papers. Toggle the tree-independent, year-filtered vector pull: without it, \emph{zero} 2026
papers ever appear; with it, ${\sim}1.9$/topic at no cost. A correctness fix, shipped on.
\rerun{\textbf{More} relevant after today: the un-treed delta grew by 27k and this channel is
what surfaces it. Cheap confirm-run recommended.}

\subsection*{03-06 landmark selection}
Older generation-side sibling of 02-04 (40-paper scale): the win is the relevance filter, not
the count; \texttt{llm40} wins at zero size increase; bigger adds nothing.
\rerun{Redundant with 02-04/02-05; historical.}

\subsection*{03-07 graph representation}
How the subgraph is serialized for the writer: structure axis (flat/adjacency/DOT/subtree/
JSON/matrix), then role tags, extracted content, aliasing layered on the winner. Production
digest = adjacency list + extracted content + roles + aliases; extracted content is the single
biggest lever ($0.75$ pairwise net); winner tops every axis (4.906, pairwise 7/1/0) at the
fewest tokens.
\rerun{Current and load-bearing for the writer; rerun only if the digest changes.}

\subsection*{03-08 judge loop}
Test-time compute: cap judge-driven gap-expansion rounds at $\{1,2,4,6\}$. Rounds double
landmarks ($80\to170$) and frontier coverage ($37\to74$); gold recall $0.81\to0.96$; pairwise
rounds-6 vs rounds-1 = $6/0/2$ with zero axis losses. The thinking judge self-stops
(${\sim}3.4$ rounds at cap 6), so the cap is a ceiling --- shipped as the effort tiers.
\rerun{Current deep-path behavior; deep-cost; rerun for the paper only.}

\subsection*{03-09 critique depth}
The documented null: per-paper full-PDF critiques at $k\in\{0,4,8\}$. $k{=}4$ nudges recall
($0.88\to0.92$) and grounding; $k{=}8$ gives it all back and faithfulness slips monotonically
($0.99\to0.95\to0.94$); pairwise $k8$ vs $k0$ = $2/2/4$. The deep loop's win is the rounds
(03-08), not depth. Kept $k{=}4$.
\rerun{A null that saved a cost sink; leave it.}

\subsection*{03-10 vision figures}
Toggle the figure gallery (vision model reads landmark PDFs, extracts diagrams/plots, captions
verified against the image). Text judge unchanged (it cannot see images); the gallery adds
${\sim}13$ verified figures at $0.91$ caption-faithfulness. Additive enrichment, shipped as an
option (prod now also role-gates figures --- slightly stricter than the measured config).
\rerun{Current; only if you want the role-gated config re-measured.}

\subsection*{03-11 hub suppression}
Keep Attention/BERT/Adam from colonizing niche reviews (Attention: 27/27). Swept four
data-derived genericness signals (lift, intent background-ratio, \texttt{pop\_bg},
\texttt{pr\_ratio}): no structural silver bullet, because a hub genuinely \emph{used} in a
niche cannot be removed without recall cost --- ``generic here'' is partly narrative. Shipped
fix lives in the writer (compose guard), A/B-validated: generic-hub mentions
$3/1/3/1 \to 0/0/0/0$ on the test topic with niche coverage preserved.
\rerun{Compose guard is current prod; the sweep half is research context.}

\subsection*{deep-research (flagship)}
The holistic before/after: old single-pass default vs the full deep pipeline, 8 topics, judged
pairwise on \emph{full untruncated} reports by three disjoint judge families: $8/0/0$ on
overall and grounding, unanimous (96 axis-verdicts, zero baseline wins; sign test
$p \approx 2\cdot(1/2)^{8} \approx 0.008$ per judge). Objectively: 9.0 2026-papers/topic vs 0,
$2.4\times$ frontier coverage, $1.5\times$ grounded citations, 13 verified figures. An earlier
16k-char judge cap saw half of each deep report and reported a softer $5/2/1$ --- the
truncation artifact worth remembering.
\rerun{``After'' side already re-ran on the current stack (h2h); ``before'' is frozen by
definition. Refresh only for headline numbers.}

\section{External evaluation: the survey-qrels benchmark (04-*, NEW 07-17)}

\noindent Motivation (user call: ``hand-curated gold is unscientific''): the 27-topic battery
holds only 19 canonical + 13 spine ids \emph{total} --- a regression gate, not a
precision/recall claim. The field's answer (probed with GraphIV itself: LitSearch, PaSa,
``citation lists are not a ground truth'') is reference-list silver gold + LLM adjudication.
Scripts live in the session scratchpad (\texttt{survey\_qrels.py}, \texttt{score\_survey.py},
\texttt{recall\_at\_freq.py}, \texttt{compare\_ablate.py}, \texttt{traj\_vs\_ppr.py}); the
retrieval cap flag is in the repo (\texttt{--set-max-date} / \texttt{--set-exclude}).

\subsection*{04-01 survey-qrels pilot}
A survey paper is a human expert who already did our task: read a topic, picked the papers
that matter. So: take a survey's resolved reference list from our own citation table as the
answer key (80--250 in-corpus ids each), derive a question from the survey, hide the survey,
ask GraphIV, count how many refs come back in the pool. Weight by how often the survey
\emph{mentions} each ref (the mention rows already exist in the citation table): one-off cites
are background noise; refs mentioned $\geq 3$ times are what the survey is about. Report the
whole recall-vs-mention-frequency \emph{curve}, not one number. Pilot ($3\times$ Jan-2024 CS
surveys, fast tier): all curves rise monotonically and hit $1.0$ at $N \geq 5$--$8$ mentions;
misses concentrate in the 1-mention tail. Design lessons: derive questions from the survey
\emph{abstract} (title-only under-scopes --- the 3DGS misses were avatars/SLAM subtopics the
narrow question never asked about); survey selection uses citation count as a \emph{floor}
(${\sim}15$--$20$) + subfield$\times$year stratification, never top-cited ranking
(age / mega-topic / hub-reward bias).
\rerun{Pilot done; the ${\sim}20$-survey stratified battery is the next build.}

\subsection*{04-02 time-capped retrieval (``no newer than the survey'')}
User-mandated: corpus \emph{and clock} are frozen at the survey's publication month
(\texttt{--set-max-date YYMM}: a Store-level filter across all six pool channels + deep-loop
gap injection; \texttt{now} is anchored so per-topic frontier detection time-travels), and the
survey itself is blocklisted (\texttt{--set-exclude} --- otherwise citation drill walks the
survey's own reference edges, i.e.\ copies the answer key; it leaked into the pool in every
uncapped pilot run). Validation (diffusion-SR): leak gone, 0 postdating landmarks, core recall
$0.842$ capped vs $0.895$ uncapped --- the delta is two \emph{pre-cap} papers reachable only
via post-cap intermediaries, exactly the contamination the cap removes. None of the comparable
works (LitSearch / PaSa / 2605.29234) caps explicitly: a claimable methodological novelty.
\rerun{Shipped in the repo, both run paths, lint-clean.}

\subsection*{04-03 does the benchmark discriminate?}
A benchmark is only an instrument if it separates configs we understand. Ablations through the
capped diffusion-SR survey: \texttt{no\_graph} leaves pool metrics byte-identical (correct
null --- it cannot touch the pool) while landmark-core drops $0.263 \to 0.158$ (graph ranking
${\approx}40\%$ of core selection); \texttt{no\_frontier\_pull} drops tail recall
$0.518 \to 0.473$ (detected); \texttt{no\_cluster} \emph{raised} R@2/R@3
($0.65 \to 0.775$ / $0.842 \to 0.895$) on a smaller pool --- the topic-tree refinement may be
evicting mid-importance refs. One-topic surprise, not a conclusion; the battery decides. Pool
metrics are deterministic (no LLM in pool assembly), so pool deltas are real.
\rerun{Instrument validated on one topic; the \texttt{no\_cluster} flag is an open battery question.}

\subsection*{04-04 trajectory vs PPR on the external instrument}
First real client of the benchmark: does the magnet-walk sampler beat PPR on qrels neither was
tuned against? The first attempt was \textbf{invalid} and taught the tier rule: fast tier skips
reader grading $\Rightarrow$ the sampler's rel$\geq 2$ magnet set is empty $\Rightarrow$ it
silently degrades to a blind walk (it swapped Score-SDE/ADM/DDIM out for 2016 CNN papers ---
the degraded selector, not the algorithm). Selection A/Bs must run standard/deep; fast is
pool-metrics-only. Suggested guard (not yet coded): warn + fall back to \texttt{ppr} when
\texttt{rank=trajectory} sees no grades. Corrected run (standard, $m{=}80$, grades cached so
both arms see identical pools + grades): \textbf{trajectory wins landmark core-ref recall 6/6
cells} across the three surveys --- lm@3: $0.526/0.421$, $0.304/0.261$, $0.381/0.333$; lm@1 a
wash; rep@3 tied except a 3DGS dip ($0.130$ vs $0.261$, compose-stage, watch in the battery).
Independent-instrument validation on top of the internal-battery Pareto.
\rerun{Done; the $+1/+2$-paper margins need the 20-survey battery for intervals.}

\subsection*{04-05 (planned) full battery + adjudication + human calibration}
(a) ${\sim}20$ surveys, citation-floor + stratified, abstract-derived questions via the map
LLM, all capped, both ranks, standard tier (${\sim}2$ overnights). (b) LLM adjudication of
uncited landmarks by a judge \emph{disjoint} from the pipeline's reader (GLM / GPT-OSS, not
Gemma --- no grading your own homework); per 2605.29234 only ${\sim}51\%$ of human citations
clear a neutral relevance bar, so ``not in refs'' $\neq$ wrong. (c) Doomscroll labeler:
${\sim}100$--$200$ (question, paper) pairs labeled by the user once; if judge--user agreement
matches human--human agreement (the Bing protocol), the judge scales to the rest and the paper
gets an inter-annotator number.
