\section*{What this is}
The flagship demonstration --- \emph{not} a single-lever ablation but the holistic before/after of the
whole system, over the deep panel. \textbf{\texttt{before-2023}} mimics the old default (single-pass,
raw in-degree, recency$=0$, frontier-pull off); \textbf{\texttt{after-deep}} is the full deployed
pipeline with every shipped lever on (deep judge-loop, PPR, recency$=0.5$, frontier-pull, critique,
vision figures). The long md/tex reviews $+$ figure galleries under \texttt{results/runs/} are the
primary artifact; the table quantifies the jump. Scored with the 4-axis judge, faithfulness, and an
order-debiased pairwise.

\section*{The jump}
\textbf{The deployed pipeline is preferred over the old default \pairOverall{} (W/T/L) on overall ---
and \pairGround{} on grounding (a clean sweep, win-rate 1.0).} Concretely it surfaces \afterDelta{}
2026 papers per topic where the old default surfaced \emph{zero}, triples frontier coverage
($\beforeFront\rightarrow\afterFront$), writes a full review ($\beforeWords\rightarrow\afterWords$
words vs an abstract-length blurb), nearly doubles grounded citations, and ships \afterFig{} verified
figures (caption-faithfulness 0.93) where the old default had none. \textbf{Honest nuance:} gold
landmark recall dips slightly ($0.96\rightarrow0.85$) --- the old lineage-only pipeline over-indexes on
pre-2026 canonical papers, while the deep pipeline reallocates attention toward the frontier; the
pairwise judge nonetheless prefers the deep review almost unanimously, so the trade is clearly worth
it. \textbf{This is the artifact that gets deployed.} \textbf{Caveat:} the absolute judge axes are
compressed and near-ceiling for both; the discriminating signal is the pairwise (1.0 grounding, 0.85
overall) plus the objective coverage/length/figure deltas.
