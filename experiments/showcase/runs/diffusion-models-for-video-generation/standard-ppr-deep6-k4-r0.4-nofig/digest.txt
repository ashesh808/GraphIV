arXiv:2006.11239:
NOVELTY: Establishes the mathematical equivalence between diffusion models, denoising score matching, and Langevin dynamics via the $\epsilon$-prediction parameterization.
STRENGTHS: Achieves state-of-the-art image synthesis on CIFAR10 with an FID of 3.17 and IS of 9.46.
WEAKNESSES: Entirely limited to static image synthesis; provides no framework or methodology for temporal modeling or video data.
RELEVANCE: Establishes the foundational $\epsilon$-prediction training objective and diffusion framework upon which video generation models are built.
arXiv:2112.10752:
NOVELTY: Decoupling perceptual compression (autoencoder) from semantic generation by training diffusion models in a low-dimensional, learned latent space rather than pixel space.
STRENGTHS: Significant reduction in computational requirements while achieving SOTA results, such as an FID of 5.11 on CelebA-HQ.
WEAKNESSES: Excessive downsampling ($f$) causes information loss and stagnating fidelity; the model remains constrained by the sequential nature of diffusion sampling.
RELEVANCE: Establishes the latent-space framework and cross-attention conditioning necessary to scale diffusion models to the high-dimensional complexity of video.
arXiv:2603.17398:

STRENGTHS: High parameter efficiency (25.8M parameters, 16x fewer than AnimateDiff) and the finding that implicit temporal regularization via denoising loss outperforms explicit consistency losses.
WEAKNESSES: Lower generative quality (FID/FVD) compared to larger models and limited output scale (8 frames at 256
arXiv:2603.09721:
NOVELTY: Matrix Attention, a frame-level temporal mechanism that uses matrix-native operations and Frobenius inner products to attend across entire frames rather than individual tokens.
STRENGTHS: FrameDiT-H achieves SOTA results across multiple benchmarks, notably a 39% FVD improvement over Latte on FaceForensics, while maintaining efficiency comparable to Local Factorized attention.
WEAKNESSES: The Global-only variant (FrameDiT-G) fails to maintain temporal coherence, producing frames that resemble independent images, suggesting Matrix Attention cannot yet replace local motion priors.
RELEVANCE: Provides a high-efficiency alternative to the trade-off between expensive Full 3D attention and the motion-limited Local Factorized attention in video diffusion models.
