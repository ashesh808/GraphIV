arXiv:2210.02303:
NOVELTY: A cascaded video diffusion architecture using interleaved spatial (SSR) and temporal (TSR) super-resolution models, combined with video-image joint training and v-parameterization to mitigate temporal color shifts.
STRENGTHS: Demonstrates significant scaling benefits, where increasing base model parameters from 500M to 5.6B improves both FVD and CLIP scores (Fig 11), and achieves an 18x sampling speedup via progressive distillation (Table 1).
