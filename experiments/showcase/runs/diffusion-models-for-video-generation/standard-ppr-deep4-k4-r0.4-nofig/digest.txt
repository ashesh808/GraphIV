DEEP CRITIQUES (full-text peer review of key papers):
arXiv:2006.11239:
NOVELTY: Establishes the mathematical equivalence between diffusion models, denoising score matching, and Langevin dynamics via the $\epsilon$-prediction parameterization.
STRENGTHS: Achieves state-of-the-art image synthesis on CIFAR10 with an FID of 3.17 and IS of 9.46.
WEAKNESSES: Entirely limited to static image synthesis; provides no framework or methodology for temporal modeling or video data.
RELEVANCE: Establishes the foundational $\epsilon$-prediction training objective and diffusion framework upon which video generation models are built.
arXiv:2112.10752:
NOVELTY: Decoupling perceptual compression (autoencoder) from semantic generation by training diffusion models in a low-dimensional, learned latent space rather than pixel space.
STRENGTHS: Significant reduction in computational requirements while achieving SOTA results, such as an FID of 5.11 on CelebA-HQ.
WEAKNESSES: Excessive downsampling ($f$) causes information loss and stagnating fidelity; the model remains constrained by the sequential nature of diffusion sampling.
RELEVANCE: Establishes the latent-space framework and cross-attention conditioning necessary to scale diffusion models to the high-dimensional complexity of video.
arXiv:2604.15911:
NOVELTY: First dedicated survey focusing exclusively on video diffusion efficiency, introducing a four-pillar taxonomy: step distillation, efficient attention, model compression, and cache/trajectory optimization.
STRENGTHS: Provides a rigorous mathematical framework and a deployment-oriented categorization that distinguishes between reducing NFE (sampling depth) and reducing per-step overhead (computational complexity).
WEAKNESSES: Identifies a critical lack of inherently stable objectives for long-horizon generation, noting that current progress relies heavily on "stability engineering" and "staged training" rather than unified algorithmic solutions.
RELEVANCE: Directly addresses the review question by mapping the technical landscape of methods used to mitigate the multiplicative costs of spatial resolution, temporal length, and iterative denoising.
arXiv:2605.17248:
NOVELTY: Establishes I2V as a standalone subject rather than a sub-topic of T2V, introducing a unique two-level taxonomy (architecture and training paradigm) and distilling four core technical design pillars.
STRENGTHS: Provides an exhaustive categorization of conditioning modalities (image, text, motion, audio, camera, and multi-condition) and a comprehensive comparison of evaluation metrics, including the VBench-I2V score.
WEAKNESSES: As a survey, it lacks original empirical results or a new proposed model; it identifies a "reproducibility gap" caused by the rise of proprietary datasets but offers no technical solution.
RELEVANCE: Directly addresses the review question by providing a systematic framework to organize the fragmented I2V literature into a cohesive technical landscape.
