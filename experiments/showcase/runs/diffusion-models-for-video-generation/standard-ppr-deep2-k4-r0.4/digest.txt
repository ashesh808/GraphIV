DEEP CRITIQUES (full-text peer review of key papers):
arXiv:2603.09721:
NOVELTY: Matrix Attention, a frame-level temporal mechanism that uses matrix-native operations and Frobenius inner products to attend across entire frames rather than individual tokens.
STRENGTHS: FrameDiT-H achieves SOTA results across multiple benchmarks, notably a 39% FVD improvement over Latte on FaceForensics, while maintaining efficiency comparable to Local Factorized attention.
WEAKNESSES: The Global-only variant (FrameDiT-G) fails to maintain temporal coherence, producing frames that resemble independent images, suggesting Matrix Attention cannot yet replace local motion priors.
RELEVANCE: Provides a high-efficiency alternative to the trade-off between expensive Full 3D attention and the motion-limited Local Factorized attention in video diffusion models.
arXiv:2604.15911:
NOVELTY: First dedicated survey focusing exclusively on video diffusion efficiency, introducing a four-pillar taxonomy: step distillation, efficient attention, model compression, and cache/trajectory optimization.
STRENGTHS: Provides a rigorous mathematical framework and a deployment-oriented categorization that distinguishes between reducing NFE (sampling depth) and reducing per-step overhead (computational complexity).
WEAKNESSES: Identifies a critical lack of inherently stable objectives for long-horizon generation, noting that current progress relies heavily on "stability engineering" and "staged training" rather than unified algorithmic solutions.
RELEVANCE: Directly addresses the review question by mapping the technical landscape of methods used to mitigate the multiplicative costs of spatial resolution, temporal length, and iterative denoising.
arXiv:2006.11239:
NOVELTY: Establishes the mathematical equivalence between diffusion probabilistic models, denoising score matching, and Langevin dynamics via a simplified $\epsilon$-prediction parameterization.
STRENGTHS: Achieves state-of-the-art sample quality on CIFAR10 with an FID score of 3.17.
WEAKNESSES: Fails to produce competitive log-likelihoods compared to other likelihood-based models and is limited to static image synthesis.
RELEVANCE: Provides the foundational mathematical framework and $\epsilon$-prediction objective that underpins the current state-of-the-art in all diffusion-based generative tasks, including video.
