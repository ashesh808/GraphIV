arXiv:2601.12538:
NOVELTY: Establishes a unified "Agentic Reasoning" paradigm that distinguishes between foundational (single-agent), self-evolving (feedback/memory), and collective (multi-agent) layers, further bifurcating methods into in-context orchestration versus post-training optimization.

arXiv:2601.12560:
NOVELTY: Proposes a unified, architecture-centric taxonomy (Perception, Brain, Planning, Action, Tool Use, Collaboration) that shifts focus from application-based surveys to engineering-focused design patterns like "flow engineering" and standardized connectivity (MCP).

STRENGTHS: Provides a rigorous mathematical formalization of the agentic control loop using a POMDP framework, specifically defining the interaction between perception ($\Phi$), memory ($\mu$), cognitive planning ($\Psi$), and action policy ($\pi$).

RELEVANCE: Advances the review of LLM agents by bridging the gap between high-level cognitive methodologies and practical systems engineering, specifically regarding tool standardization and controllable orchestration.

arXiv:2201.11903:
NOVELTY: Introduces "chain-of-thought prompting," a few-shot method that provides intermediate natural language reasoning steps in exemplars to elicit complex reasoning without fine-tuning.

STRENGTHS: Demonstrates that CoT is an emergent ability of scale, with PaLM 540B achieving state-of-the-art accuracy on the GSM8K benchmark, surpassing fine-tuned models.

WEAKNESSES: The method is highly dependent on model scale (ineffective for models <100B parameters) and lacks a guarantee of logical correctness, as models can produce incorrect reasoning paths.

RELEVANCE: Establishes a foundational prompting paradigm for LLM agents to decompose multi-step tasks, providing a mechanism for increased computation and interpretability in tool-use and reasoning workflows.
