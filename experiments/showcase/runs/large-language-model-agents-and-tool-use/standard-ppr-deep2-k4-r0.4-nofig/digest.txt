DEEP CRITIQUES (full-text peer review of key papers):
arXiv:2201.11903:
NOVELTY: Introduces "chain-of-thought prompting," a few-shot method that elicits intermediate reasoning steps to unlock complex reasoning abilities in large language models without fine-tuning.

STRENGTHS: Demonstrates that CoT is an emergent property of scale, with PaLM 540B achieving state-of-the-art accuracy on the GSM8K benchmark, surpassing fine-tuned models.

WEAKNESSES: The method relies on manual composition of exemplars and lacks a guarantee of logical correctness, as models can produce "fluent but illogical" reasoning paths.

RELEVANCE: Provides a foundational prompting mechanism for LLM agents to decompose multi-step tasks, directly addressing the gap between model scale and reasoning performance.
arXiv:2210.03629:
NOVELTY: Interleaves verbal reasoning traces with task-specific actions to create a closed-loop "reason-to-act" and "act-to-reason" synergy.
STRENGTHS: Demonstrates significant few-shot advantages in interactive environments, outperforming imitation/RL methods by 34% in ALFWorld and 10% in WebShop.
WEAKNESSES: Susceptible to repetitive reasoning loops and highly dependent on external tool quality, with 23% of HotpotQA errors resulting from non-informative search results.
RELEVANCE: Provides a framework for grounding LLM reasoning in external environments, addressing the hallucination and error propagation issues inherent in standalone Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning.
arXiv:2601.12538:
NOVELTY: Establishes a unified "Agentic Reasoning" paradigm that distinguishes between foundational (single-agent), self-evolving (feedback/memory), and collective (multi-agent) layers, further bifurcating methods into in-context orchestration versus post-training optimization.

STRENGTHS: Provides a rigorous mathematical formalization of the "think-act" structure using a POMDP framework, explicitly factorizing the policy into internal reasoning ($\pi_{reason}$) and external execution ($\pi_{exec}$).

WEAKNESSES: The distinction between "in-context" and "post-training" is occasionally blurred in the taxonomy; for instance, the paper categorizes "Self-evolving" as a layer but also discusses "Parametric Adaptation" (post-training) as a mechanism, creating potential overlap in the hierarchical classification.

RELEVANCE: Advances the review of LLM agents by shifting the focus from mere architectural components (memory, tools) to the underlying cognitive process of reasoning that drives agentic behavior.
arXiv:2604.00835:
NOVELTY: Shifts focus from static task/architecture taxonomies to a methodological evolutionary view based on the progression of optimization signals (in-context, supervised, and reward-driven).
STRENGTHS: Provides a structured three-paradigm taxonomy (Fig. 1) and a rigorous multi-dimensional comparison against existing surveys (Table 1) to justify its unique positioning.
WEAKNESSES: Claims to analyze "trade-offs" and "failure modes" between paradigms but lacks empirical evidence or quantitative comparative data to substantiate these qualitative assertions.
RELEVANCE: Advances the review by transforming fragmented literature into a coherent developmental trajectory, bridging the gap between prompting, fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning.
