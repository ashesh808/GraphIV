DEEP CRITIQUES (full-text peer review of key papers):
arXiv:2006.11239:
NOVELTY: Establishes the capability of diffusion models for high-quality image synthesis and connects them to denoising score matching and Langevin dynamics.
STRENGTHS: Achieves a state-of-the-art FID of 3.17 on CIFAR10 using the $\epsilon$-prediction parameterization ($L_{simple}$).
WEAKNESSES: Log-likelihoods are not competitive with other likelihood-based generative models.
RELEVANCE: Provides the empirical proof required to position diffusion models as a viable alternative to GANs for image generation.
arXiv:2605.17248:
NOVELTY: Establishes I2V as a standalone subject via a two-level taxonomy (architecture and training paradigm) and a four-pillar design analysis (encoding, temporal modeling, noise priors, and upsampling).
STRENGTHS: Provides a highly granular categorization of conditioning modalities (audio, camera, motion, etc.) and systematic comparisons of datasets (Table I) and metrics (Table II).
WEAKNESSES: Lacks original empirical benchmarking or a proposed technical framework to mitigate the "reproducibility gap" caused by proprietary datasets.
RELEVANCE: Advances the review by isolating the specific technical constraints of I2V—identity preservation and temporal coherence—from general video generation.
arXiv:2112.10752:
NOVELTY: Decoupling perceptual compression (via autoencoders) from semantic generation (via diffusion) and introducing cross-attention for multi-modal conditioning.
STRENGTHS: Significant computational efficiency; LDM-8 achieves a 38 FID gap improvement over pixel-based LDM-1 after 2M training steps.
WEAKNESSES: High sensitivity to the downsampling factor $f$; excessive compression leads to information loss and stagnating fidelity.
RELEVANCE: Provides a scalable, resource-efficient framework for high-resolution and text-conditioned image generation by operating in latent space.
