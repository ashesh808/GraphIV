DEEP CRITIQUES (full-text peer review of key papers):
arXiv:2006.11239:
NOVELTY: Establishes the capability of diffusion models for high-quality image synthesis and connects them to denoising score matching and Langevin dynamics.
STRENGTHS: Achieves a state-of-the-art FID of 3.17 on CIFAR10 using the $\epsilon$-prediction parameterization ($L_{simple}$).
WEAKNESSES: Log-likelihoods are not competitive with other likelihood-based generative models.
RELEVANCE: Provides the empirical proof required to position diffusion models as a viable alternative to GANs for image generation.
arXiv:2011.13456:
NOVELTY: Unifies score-based modeling and DDPM into a continuous-time framework using Stochastic Differential Equations (SDEs), introducing the probability flow ODE for exact likelihood and deterministic sampling.
STRENGTHS: Achieves state-of-the-art performance on CIFAR-10 with an Inception Score of 9.89 and FID of 2.20, while setting a record likelihood of 2.99 bits/dim.
WEAKNESSES: Sampling remains significantly slower than GANs, and the framework introduces a high number of hyperparameters related to SDE selection and sampler tuning.
RELEVANCE: Provides a mathematical foundation that generalizes discrete diffusion processes, enabling new sampling algorithms (Predictor-Corrector) and controllable generation (inpainting/colorization).
arXiv:2505.20984:
NOVELTY: Reinterprets the compression process itself as a forward diffusion path via quantization scaling, enabling a single model to perform rate-variable generative reconstruction without Gaussian noise initialization.

STRENGTHS: Achieves superior perceptual performance and statistical fidelity (FID/KID) compared to existing diffusion-based (CDC) and GAN-based (HiFiC) methods, while requiring only 2 sampling steps.

WEAKNESSES: The claim of "organic integration" is partially undermined by the reliance on a pretrained VAE and a separate entropy model, and the theoretical derivation of the reverse SDE relies on an approximation of quantization as additive uniform noise.

RELEVANCE: Advances the review by shifting diffusion models from "post-processing" tools to an intrinsic component of the compression-decompression loop.
