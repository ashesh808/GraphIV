DEEP CRITIQUES (full-text peer review of key papers):
arXiv:2010.02502:
NOVELTY: Generalization of the forward diffusion process from Markovian to non-Markovian trajectories that share the same training objective, enabling deterministic, implicit generative modeling.
STRENGTHS: Achieves 10× to 50× faster sampling in wall-clock time compared to DDPMs while maintaining high quality (e.g., CIFAR10 FID of 4.16 at 100 steps) and enabling semantic latent space interpolation.
WEAKNESSES: Sample quality inherently trades off with computational speed, and the empirical investigation of non-Gaussian forward processes is deferred to future work.
RELEVANCE: Directly addresses the primary bottleneck of diffusion models—slow sampling—by providing a method to accelerate inference using pretrained models without retraining.
arXiv:2011.13456:
NOVELTY: Unifies score-based modeling and DDPM into a continuous-time framework using Stochastic Differential Equations (SDEs), introducing the probability flow ODE for exact likelihood and deterministic sampling.
STRENGTHS: Achieves state-of-the-art performance on CIFAR-10 with an Inception Score of 9.89 and FID of 2.20, while setting a record likelihood of 2.99 bits/dim.
WEAKNESSES: Sampling remains significantly slower than GANs, and the framework introduces a high number of hyperparameters related to SDE selection and sampler tuning.
RELEVANCE: Provides a mathematical foundation that generalizes discrete diffusion processes, enabling new sampling algorithms (Predictor-Corrector) and controllable generation (inpainting/colorization).
arXiv:2605.18736:
NOVELTY: A spectral-domain progressive resolution framework that uses a principled noise expansion mechanism and a power-spectrum-derived schedule to exploit the spectral autoregression property of diffusion models.
STRENGTHS: Demonstrates significant efficiency gains, achieving up to 7$\times$ speedup in image generation and 2.5$\times$ in video generation while outperforming spatial acceleration baselines (RALU, Bottleneck) in the speed-quality tradeoff.
WEAKNESSES: Implicitly assumes that pretrained models can generalize to the intermediate, potentially unseen resolutions and continuous schedules used during the progressive denoising trajectory.
RELEVANCE: Directly addresses the scaling crisis of Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) by reducing token counts during early denoising steps where high-frequency computation is redundant.
arXiv:2606.04299:
NOVELTY: Replaces learned score functions with an analytical, closed-form denoiser derived from the image's internal patch distribution.
STRENGTHS: Achieves superior diversity (LPIPS Div. 0.50 vs. 0.41 for SinDiffusion) and extreme scalability (1 GP in 13.9 minutes).
WEAKNESSES: Relies on coarse-to-fine blending heuristics to compensate for the lack of inherent global semantic modeling in patch-based distributions.
RELEVANCE: Introduces a training-free paradigm to diffusion models, bypassing the heavy optimization bottleneck typical of single
arXiv:2006.11239:
NOVELTY: Establishes the mathematical equivalence between diffusion models and denoising score matching/Langevin dynamics through a specific $\epsilon$-prediction parameterization.
STRENGTHS: Demonstrates high-quality image synthesis, achieving a state-of-the-art FID of 3.17 on CIFAR10.
WEAKNESSES: Fails to produce competitive log-likelihoods compared to other likelihood-based generative models.
RELEVANCE: Provides the foundational proof and simplified training objective ($L_{simple}$) required to make diffusion models viable for high-quality image generation.
