arXiv:2111.00396:
NOVELTY: Introduces the Structured State Space (S4) model using a Normal Plus Low-Rank (NPLR) parameterization of the state matrix $A$, enabling stable diagonalization and efficient computation via a Cauchy kernel.
STRENGTHS: Solves the 16k-length Path-X task (88% accuracy) where all prior models failed, and achieves 60× faster generation than Transformers on WikiText-103.
WEAKNESSES: Susceptible to numerical instability if $A$ has eigenvalues in the right half-plane; ablation studies indicate performance is driven primarily by HiPPO initialization rather than the NPLR parameterization itself.
RELEVANCE: Provides an $O(N+L)$ complexity framework that unifies continuous, recurrent, and convolutional representations to handle extremely long-range dependencies across multiple modalities.
arXiv:2605.15311:
NOVELTY: Integration of basis function expansion into deep SSM architectures to parameterize $A[t]$, $B[t]$, and $C[t]$ matrices, enabling continuous time-varying dynamics without explicit switching mode specification.

STRENGTHS: Demonstrates superior performance in non-stationary tasks, specifically achieving up to 19.6 dB SI-SNR in speech denoising compared to 14.2 dB for time-invariant models (Table 3).

WEAKNESSES: The stability guarantee relies on a simplifying assumption that $A[t]$ is diagonal and basis functions are bounded; the paper lacks empirical validation for non-diagonal $A[t]$ stability.

RELEVANCE: Advances the review by providing a mechanism for SSMs to handle non-stationary/switching dynamics through learnable temporal basis functions rather than just input-dependent gating.
arXiv:2512.23824:
NOVELTY: Integration of Multi-Resolution Analysis (MRA) via trainable dilated convolutions and scale-dependent $\bar{A}$ initialization to control effective memory per resolution.
STRENGTHS: Significant performance gains on hierarchical tasks; MS-SSM (S6) achieves 63.04% on ListOps, nearly doubling Mamba's 38.02%.
WEAKNESSES: Lacks formal theoretical justification for the scale-dependent initialization scheme and provides only qualitative complexity claims rather than a detailed FLOPs/parameter comparison.
RELEVANCE: Directly addresses the single-scale dependency and limited memory limitations of current SSMs, proposing a hierarchical architecture for complex sequence modeling.
