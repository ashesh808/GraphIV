DEEP CRITIQUES (full-text peer review of key papers):
arXiv:2110.14168:
NOVELTY: Introduces GSM8K, a high-diversity grade school math dataset, and a verification framework that uses a separate model to rank multiple sampled completions.

STRENGTHS: Demonstrates that verification scales more efficiently with data than fine-tuning, providing a performance boost equivalent to a 30x increase in model size.

WEAKNESSES: Test-time compute benefits are non-monotonic; increasing the number of completions beyond 400 leads to performance degradation due to the risk of finding adversarial solutions that fool the verifier.

RELEVANCE: Establishes a foundational method for improving reasoning via test-time search and ranking, directly addressing the limitations of autoregressive generation in multi-step tasks.
arXiv:2603.07159:
NOVELTY: Shifts uncertainty-driven selection from token-level or full-trajectory sampling to the granularity of "thoughts" (intermediate reasoning steps).
STRENGTHS: Demonstrates that self-certainty maximization matches or exceeds self-consistency under equivalent token budgets and that optimizing only the first 1–5 steps yields peak accuracy.
WEAKNESSES: The method's reliance on predefined reasoning delimiters (e.g., double line breaks) to segment thoughts may not generalize to models without structured CoT formatting.
RELEVANCE: Advances test-time compute research by identifying an efficient, model-internal scaling strategy that prioritizes early-stage reasoning decisions.
arXiv:2605.10344:
NOVELTY: Introduction of hierarchical memory (Experience vs. Guideline banks) and a hybrid reward RL scheme that explicitly incentivizes experience utilization and strategy novelty.
STRENGTHS: Significant performance gains on IMO-AnswerBench, specifically increasing Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507 Pass@1 from 53.5% to 73.5%.
WEAKNESSES: Heavy reliance on a "cold-start" dataset distilled from a teacher model (DeepSeek-V3.2) and high computational overhead from running multiple specialized agents and verification passes across many iterations.
RELEVANCE: Advances the review by demonstrating how structured multi-agent coordination and memory management can optimize the efficiency of test-time compute scaling.
arXiv:2201.11903:
NOVELTY: Introduces "chain-of-thought prompting," a method to elicit multi-step reasoning by providing ⟨input, reasoning step, output⟩ triples in few-shot exemplars, rather than just input-output pairs.

STRENGTHS: Demonstrates that CoT is an emergent ability of scale, showing that PaLM 540B achieves new state-of-the-art performance on the GSM8K benchmark, surpassing fine-tuned models.

WEAKNESSES: The method's efficacy is strictly tied to large model scales (~100B+ parameters), as smaller models produce fluent but illogical reasoning paths that fail to improve performance.

RELEVANCE: Establishes that increasing test-time compute (via intermediate token generation) can unlock reasoning capabilities that are otherwise inaccessible through standard prompting.
