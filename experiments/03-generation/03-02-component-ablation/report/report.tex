\section*{What we did}
The classic ablation: knock out one pipeline component at a time (control $=$ \texttt{full}) and read
the \emph{full metric vector} $+$ an independent 4-axis LLM judge (Gemma-4-26B: grounding / coverage /
lineage / coherence) $+$ a faithfulness tripwire, over the 27-topic battery. Conditions:
\texttt{indegree} (raw in-degree, no PPR/frontier reservation), \texttt{no\_cluster} (drop the
topic-tree leaf anchor), \texttt{no\_graph} (vector-only, no citation subgraph), \texttt{no\_fulltext}
(abstracts only). The point is to reproduce the legacy finding that \textbf{no single metric can be
trusted}.

\section*{Conclusion}
\textbf{\texttt{no\_graph} is the gameable-single-metric artifact}: it posts the \emph{highest}
frontier count (\ngFrontier{}, pure similarity floods recent papers) yet \textbf{\ngEdges{} citation
edges}, near-zero landmark recall, and the \emph{worst} judge overall (\ngJudge{}) --- chronology and
canonical coverage are destroyed while a surface metric improves. \textbf{\texttt{indegree}} starves
the frontier (\indFrontier{} vs \ngFrontier{} structure; full $=$ healthy middle) --- raw in-degree
favors old hubs, the age-vs-citation bias PPR$+$frontier reservation fixes. \textbf{\texttt{no\_cluster}}
cuts citation density ($\fullEdges\rightarrow\ncEdges$ edges): the topic-tree leaf anchor keeps the
pool connected and on-topic. \textbf{\texttt{no\_fulltext}} keeps structure but halves landmarks and
lowers the judge --- abstracts carry less to synthesize. \textbf{Faithfulness $\approx 0.95$
everywhere} --- a correctness tripwire, not a lever-ranking score. \textbf{The thesis:} the full
system is the only configuration that keeps \emph{all} axes healthy at once; any knockout that wins
one metric loses others catastrophically. \textbf{Caveat:} judge-overall is compressed ($\sim$4.6--4.9,
the known distribution collapse) and landmark\_recall is diluted by the 24 non-gold topics --- the
robust signals are edges, frontier balance, and the cross-axis pattern.
