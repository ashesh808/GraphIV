\section*{What we did}
Vary only the judge/gap-expansion round cap \texttt{max\_rounds} $\in \{1,2,4,6\}$, all on the deep
path (long-compose $+$ critique held constant, figures off to isolate the loop), over the 27-topic
battery. A thinking judge owns termination, so the cap is only a ceiling. Scored on the metric vector
$+$ 4-axis judge $+$ an order-debiased \textbf{pairwise} of \texttt{rounds-6} vs \texttt{rounds-1}.

\section*{Conclusion}
\textbf{The deep loop is a real quality lever --- spending inference-time compute on iterative
critique-and-retrieve measurably broadens the review.} Landmarks nearly double
($\rOneLm\rightarrow\rFourLm$), frontier coverage doubles, and gold recall climbs to \rFourRecall{}
(at \texttt{rounds-4}). The absolute judge axes are compressed ($\sim$4.6--4.9, the known collapse),
but the \textbf{pairwise discriminates sharply: \texttt{rounds-6} beats \texttt{rounds-1} 21--2--4 on
overall (win-rate 0.78), and 0.78 on lineage}. \textbf{The judge self-regulates}: even at cap 6 the
mean rounds actually run is $\sim$\rFourRun{}--2.8, so it stops when a topic is saturated.
\textbf{Diminishing returns after $\sim$3 rounds} (gold recall dips at \texttt{rounds-6}, within
run-to-run variance). \textbf{Ship deep with cap $\approx 4$}; the judge handles early termination.
\textbf{Caveat:} more rounds $=$ more landmarks but \emph{not} more words (length is set by
long-compose, not the loop) --- the loop adds breadth, not padding.
