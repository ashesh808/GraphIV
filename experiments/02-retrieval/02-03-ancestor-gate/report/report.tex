\section*{What we did}
\texttt{02-02} found $\sim$22\% of landmarks off-topic AND carrying high in-degree (5.4) --- famous
cross-field hubs (GPT-3 / StyleGAN under ``diffusion''). They enter through one mechanism:
\texttt{augment\_with\_ancestors} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{store.ancestors} pulls the top-120
\emph{most-cited} one-hop-back papers (ranked by citer count \emph{before} cosine), so fame beats
relevance. We gate that injection by a cosine floor, sensitivity-swept over a \textbf{0.25--0.45 grid}
(step 0.05 --- the original 0.35 came from a coarse search, so the ship decision needed plateau-vs-peak),
and measure \textbf{both} sides: \textbf{precision} (LLM \texttt{relevance\_rate} over 27 topics,
reusing the \texttt{02-02} cache) and \textbf{recall} (\texttt{landmark\_recall} +
\texttt{spine\_recall} over 3 gold topics). Only the injected ancestors are gated; the core pool and
cosine-gated \texttt{frontier\_pull} are untouched.

\section*{Conclusion}
\textbf{Recall sits on a flat plateau across 0.25--0.35} --- landmark recall $\floorRec$ and spine
$\floorSpine$ are \emph{identical} at all three floors while precision rises monotonically
($0.797 \rightarrow 0.819 \rightarrow \floorRel$; baseline $\baseRel$/$\baseRec$). The gains are not
tuned to a magic number: any gentle floor beats no floor on both metrics, and \textbf{0.35 is the
max-precision edge of the plateau} --- the principled ship point. \textbf{The cliff starts at 0.40}
(recall falls back to baseline) and \textbf{0.45 collapses it} (0.522, spine 0.6): those floors cut
genuine low-cosine foundational papers (VAE / Sohl-Dickstein sit $\sim$0.4--0.5). An earlier pass
also tested a modal-category gate at 0.45; it added $\sim$nothing and is dropped.
\textbf{Verdict: shipped} --- \texttt{augment\_with\_ancestors(min\_cosine=0.35)} is the production
default (\texttt{pipeline.py} + the harness recipe); 0 remains the ablation arm.
\textbf{Caveat:} recall is n$=3$ gold, precision n$=27$; the three-floor plateau plus the mechanism
make the no-recall-loss claim robust, but a wider gold set would harden it further.
