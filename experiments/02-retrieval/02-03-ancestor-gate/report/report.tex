\section*{What we did}
\texttt{02-02} found $\sim$22\% of landmarks off-topic AND carrying high in-degree (5.4) --- famous
cross-field hubs (GPT-3 / StyleGAN under ``diffusion''). They enter through one mechanism:
\texttt{augment\_with\_ancestors} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{store.ancestors} pulls the top-120
\emph{most-cited} one-hop-back papers (ranked by citer count \emph{before} cosine), so fame beats
relevance. We gate that injection by a cosine floor and measure \textbf{both} sides: \textbf{precision}
(LLM \texttt{relevance\_rate} over 27 topics, reusing the \texttt{02-02} cache) and \textbf{recall}
(\texttt{landmark\_recall} + \texttt{spine\_recall} over 3 gold topics). Only the injected ancestors
are gated; the core pool and cosine-gated \texttt{frontier\_pull} are untouched.

\section*{Conclusion}
The tradeoff is \textbf{floor-dependent}. \textbf{A gentle floor (0.35) is a Pareto win, not a
tradeoff}: precision $\baseRel\rightarrow\floorRel$ (robust, n$=27$) \emph{and} gold recall up
($\baseRec\rightarrow\floorRec$, spine $\rightarrow\floorSpine$). Removing clearly-off-topic ancestors
(cosine $<0.35$) frees PPR's 40 landmark slots for on-topic papers instead of famous hubs --- both
metrics rise together. \textbf{An aggressive floor (0.45) is the real tradeoff}: precision climbs to
0.896 but gold recall collapses to 0.522 (it cuts genuine low-cosine foundational papers --- VAE /
Sohl-Dickstein sit $\sim$0.4--0.5). The category gate adds $\sim$nothing (0.896 $\rightarrow$ 0.902);
drop it. \textbf{Verdict:} a \texttt{min\_cosine}$\approx$0.35 floor on
\texttt{augment\_with\_ancestors} is worth promoting to production --- a low-risk Pareto improvement.
\textbf{Caveat:} recall is n$=3$ gold, precision n$=27$; a wider gold set would harden the
no-recall-loss claim.
