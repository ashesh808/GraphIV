\section*{What we did}
\texttt{02-03} cleaned the pool with a cosine gate; this asks how to pick the final landmark set and
\textbf{whether the cap must grow}. Four arms at cap 50, over all 27 topics, scoring \emph{every}
selected paper $-2..+2$ on its \textbf{salient full text} (Gemma-4-26B @T$=0$): \texttt{sum} penalizes
drift/noise (count-confounded), \texttt{mean} $=$ per-paper quality (count-independent),
\texttt{neg2\_per\_list} $=$ outright-unrelated papers kept. Arms: \texttt{fixed50} (PPR top-50),
\texttt{gate50} (0.35 cosine gate), \texttt{llm50} (drop role$=$unrelated over PPR-80, capped 50 ---
same count), \texttt{llm\_dyn} (same filter, soft cap 75 --- more count). Scores cached per
\texttt{(topic, arxiv\_id)}, config-independent.

\section*{Conclusion --- the count doesn't matter; the filters are independent flags}
\textbf{Filtering raises both sum and mean}: \texttt{fixed50}$\rightarrow$\texttt{gate50} lifts sum
$\fixedSum\rightarrow\gateSum$ ($+42\%$) and mean $\fixedMean\rightarrow\gateMean$ ($+40\%$); the
unfiltered top-50 carries 5.6 unrelated papers per list, the gate halves that, the LLM filter zeroes
it. \textbf{Going bigger raises sum via clean relevant mass while mean holds}:
\texttt{llm50}$\rightarrow$\texttt{llm\_dyn} adds $\sim$22 papers, sum jumps
$\llmSum\rightarrow\llmdynSum$ ($+48\%$) while mean is flat ($\llmMean\rightarrow\llmdynMean$) --- the
extra papers are genuine on-topic mass, not dilution, but they make no \emph{paper} better. \textbf{So
the count is not a lever}: \texttt{sum} rises with clean count (recall-flavored); \texttt{mean} --- the
count-independent quality --- saturates at $\sim$0.88 once you filter, exactly where the compose-judge
(\texttt{landmark-selection}, in \texttt{03-generation}) said the review saturates.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{This makes cosine-gate and LLM-filter two independent on/off flags, not a tuned
size:} the 0.35 cosine gate alone gets $\sim$95\% of the mean win for free (no LLM); the LLM filter
drives residual unrelated papers from $\sim$3 to 0 (precision-max); either can be enabled without
touching the cap. \textbf{Caveat:} \texttt{sum} is count-confounded by construction; \texttt{mean} is
the fair cross-arm comparison. Downstream review quality is judged in \texttt{03-generation}.
